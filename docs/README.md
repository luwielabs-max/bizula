# Bizula

Bizula is an all-in-one business operating system built by **Luwie Labs** to help businesses manage inventory, services, sales, bookings, customers, payments, and analytics from a single platform.

Bizula is designed for:

- Retail businesses
- Service businesses
- Hybrid businesses (Retail + Services)

---

# Features

## Authentication

- Email & Password Authentication
- Google Authentication
- Apple Authentication
- Email Verification
- Password Recovery

---

## Retail

- Inventory Management
- Product Management
- Customer Orders
- Sales Tracking
- Revenue Analytics

---

## Services

- Service Management
- Online Booking Links
- Calendar
- Booking Management

---
