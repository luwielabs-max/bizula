# Business Roles

Bizula currently supports four account types.

---

## Retail

Modules

- Dashboard
- Inventory
- Sales
- Customers
- Orders
- Payments
- Settings

---

## Service

Modules

- Dashboard
- Services
- Bookings
- Calendar
- Customers
- Payments
- Settings

---

## Both

Receives every module from Retail and Service.

---

## Admin

Platform administration.

Future responsibilities include

- User Management
- Subscription Management
- Analytics
- Reports
